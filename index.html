Cook Out's New Locations in 2026: What We Know About Milton, Florida and South Boston, Virginia

By Matt Hanson, November 23, 2026

Cook Out keeps growing across the Southeast, and two new restaurants are on the map for 2026: one in Milton, Florida and one in South Boston, Virginia. Neither location has an announced opening date yet, but both have confirmed addresses and real local reporting behind them. Here is what is actually known so far, and what fans in those towns should watch for next.

Milton, Florida: 6180 Highway 90

The Milton restaurant is planned for 6180 Highway 90, in the building that formerly housed an Ollie's outlet. The property was bought in February 2026, according to reporting in the Pensacola News Journal. That purchase is the firmest fact on the record for this location: the site is secured, the address is set, and the building is a known landmark on a busy stretch of Highway 90 in Santa Rosa County, not far from Pensacola.

What is not known yet is the opening date. No opening date has been announced for the Milton restaurant. That is normal for Cook Out, which tends to confirm an opening only when construction and hiring are close to finished. Until a date appears on the official locator or in local news, any date floating around online should be treated as a guess, not a fact.

Milton makes sense as a Cook Out town. It sits on the corridor between Pensacola and the rest of the Florida Panhandle, an area where the chain already has a following, and Highway 90 carries steady local and through traffic. A Cook Out there gives Panhandle fans a closer option than driving into Pensacola, and it fits the chain's pattern of filling in gaps between its existing restaurants.

South Boston, Virginia: 3616 Old Halifax Road

The South Boston restaurant is planned for 3616 Old Halifax Road. As with Milton, no opening date has been announced yet for this location. The address is confirmed, and the restaurant is listed as a planned opening, but the timeline is still open.

South Boston is a smaller market than the chain's usual college-town and metro locations, which is part of what makes it interesting. Cook Out has built its name on value trays and late-night hours in North Carolina and Virginia, and a South Boston restaurant would extend that footprint deeper into southern Virginia, serving both the town itself and drivers passing through on the way to and from North Carolina.

What a new Cook Out usually brings

While opening dates are still unannounced, the format at a new Cook Out is predictable, because the chain rarely changes its formula. Expect a drive-thru focused restaurant with a walk-up window, a menu built around trays, and the full milkshake list. A regular tray pairs a main item with two sides and a drink, and the milkshake menu runs to more than 40 flavors, from classics like vanilla and chocolate to seasonal options. Many locations stay open late, which is a big part of the chain's appeal for night-shift workers, students, and road trippers.

Prices vary by location, so the exact tray and shake prices at Milton and South Boston will only be clear once the menu boards go up. That variation is worth remembering: a price you see quoted for a Cook Out in Raleigh or Charlotte is not a promise for a new store in Florida or Virginia. How to check before you drive

If you live near Milton or South Boston and want to be first in line, the most reliable checks are simple. First, watch the official Cook Out restaurant locator, which is the source that confirms an opening. Second, keep an eye on local news in each town, since a hiring notice or a building permit story usually appears weeks before opening day. Third, check the locations page, where I log confirmed addresses and opening dates as they are verified.

Two cautions are worth keeping in mind. One, do not trust an opening date you see on a random listings site unless it points back to the official locator or a local news report. Two, a "coming soon" sign on the building is not an opening date. Cook Out has opened restaurants on short notice before, and the gap between a finished building and the first tray served can be weeks or months.

For now, the honest summary is short: Milton, Florida at 6180 Highway 90 and South Boston, Virginia at 3616 Old Halifax Road are both real, confirmed, planned Cook Out locations, and neither has an opening date yet.
